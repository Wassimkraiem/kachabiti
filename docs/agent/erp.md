### ERP: sales pipeline, customers, till, purchasing, money

Migrations `20261016120000_erp_sales_crm`, `…121000_erp_purchasing_pos` and `…122000_erp_accounting`. NestJS routes validate with the `Zod` pipe and refuse with `ApiError` (`api/src/common/api.ts`; zod errors and known database exceptions become 4xx with a message in `DB_MESSAGES`, `lib/erp/db-messages.ts`); shared values and labels are in `lib/erp/constants.ts`; admin screens use `components/admin/erp/*` (`api()`, `useApi()`, `formatMoney()` with 3 decimals, `PageHeader`, `Kpi`, `PeriodFilter`, `AgentSelect`, dependency-free SVG `BarChart` / `BarList` / `TargetBar`). Business rules a person can change are in `erp_settings` (admin only; `site_settings` is read by the storefront), edited in Admin → Business settings and Accounting → Settings.
- **Orders pipeline.** `orders.status`: `pending` (to confirm) → `confirmed` (customer agreed) → `processing` (validated: parcel, stock out) → `shipped` → `delivered`, or `returned` (stock back) / `cancelled`; `set_order_status` stamps `confirmed_at`, `delivered_at`, `returned_at`, `cancelled_at`. Orders also carry `channel` (website, facebook, instagram, tiktok, whatsapp, phone, store, other), `campaign_id`, `assigned_to` (the agent handling it; `agent_performance` and objectives use `coalesce(assigned_to, created_by)`), `discount_amount` (total = subtotal − discount + shipping), and `location_id` (stock source; null = online location). Agents create orders in Admin → New order (`POST /api/sales/orders`, priced by `buildManualOrderLines()` in `api/src/sales/commerce.ts`), and `PATCH /api/sales/orders` assigns, confirms and sets the source. Discounts above `erp_settings.pos.max_discount_percent` need an owner or admin (also at the till).
- **Duplicates.** `find_order_duplicates(phone)` lists open orders from the same phone (`normalize_phone`: 8 Tunisian digits); the New order form and the orders list warn about them.
- **Customers (CRM).** `customers` has one live row per normalized phone; the trigger `private.link_order_customer` links every new order to it (creating it), and `sync_customer_stage` moves its `stage` with its latest order (`new` / `interested` are set by hand). Conversations are `customer_interactions` (direction in/out/note), follow-ups `crm_tasks`. `merge_customers`, `find_customer_duplicates`, `anonymize_customer` (personal-data erasure). Meta channels are optional: `POST /api/webhooks/meta` (public, signature-checked with `META_APP_SECRET`; `META_VERIFY_TOKEN`) stores incoming Messenger/Instagram/WhatsApp messages, and replies go out through `lib/erp/meta.ts` (`META_PAGE_ACCESS_TOKEN`, `WHATSAPP_TOKEN`, `WHATSAPP_PHONE_NUMBER_ID`). Owners and admins connect the Facebook Page in the Inbox (`/api/admin/meta`, Facebook Login for Business, `META_APP_ID` + `META_LOGIN_CONFIG_ID`): its token, encrypted with a key made from `AUTH_SECRET`, is the one row of `meta_connection` and wins over `META_PAGE_ACCESS_TOKEN`; webhook events of other Pages are then ignored. Without them the CRM works by hand. The same webhook stores public post comments (Page `feed`, Instagram `comments`; `parseComments()`) in `social_comments`, listed in Admin → Messenger (`/admin/customers/inbox`, `components/admin/erp/messenger.tsx`), whose Messages tab has one thread per customer and channel (`/api/crm/interactions?inbox=1`) and whose Comments tab has one per author and platform (`/api/crm/comments?threads=1`); the trigger `private.social_comment_lead` finds a Tunisian phone number in a comment (`private.find_tunisian_phone`), links or creates its customer, opens one "Call back" task and notifies the customers team. Setup steps: `docs/META.md`. Message files (`customer_interactions.attachments`) and profile photos (`customers.external_ids.<messenger|instagram>_photo`) are copied from Meta to the `documents` bucket and served by `/api/crm/media` (`api/src/crm/meta-media.ts`); `anonymize_customer` clears them and the API deletes the files. `api/src/crm/meta-import.ts` reads past conversations (both sides, deduplicated by message id; nothing from before a customer was erased or removed). In Messenger a staff member who opens an unassigned conversation claims its customer (`/api/crm/interactions/claim`, `claimCustomer` in `meta-messages.ts`); replies (text and files) are refused to other staff, owners and admins may reply or take over, the "Assigned to me" tab lists one's own (`?assigned=me`, count `mine`), and their customers' messages notify the agent as `assigned_message` (own sound and look). Saved replies (`saved_replies`, `/api/crm/replies`, `components/admin/erp/saved-replies.tsx`) are shared by the team and put into the message box. Meta ads (`api/src/social/meta-ads.controller.ts`, `/admin/marketing/meta-ads`) reads the chosen ad account with the connecting person's token (`meta_connection.user_token_encrypted`, ads_read) and joins `ad_referrals` (Meta's referral on messages) to orders within 30 days; it books nothing. The shop's own replies (message echoes) are stored as `out`; `api/src/crm/meta-health.ts` checks and repairs the connection. Scheduled posts (`scheduled_posts`, Admin → Marketing → Post calendar, `api/src/social/`) are claimed by `claim_due_posts` and never sent twice; interrupted ones fail for a person to retry. Notifications of `MESSAGE_NOTIFICATIONS` types have their own header icon and sound (`?scope=messages|other`).
- **Money is not delivery.** Each payment received is an `order_payments` row (negative: refund) with `account` cash, bank or carrier (cash on delivery Colissimo still holds); `orders.paid_amount` / `payment_status` (`pending`, `partial`, `paid`, `refunded`) follow them through a trigger — don't set them by hand. A carrier payout (`record_remittance`, `cod_remittances` with fees) clears the carrier balance. `treasury_movements` are the other cash/bank movements (opening balance, owner, loans, transfers via `record_cash_transfer`, till differences). `treasury_balances()` sums it all.
- **Till.** `pos_sessions` per shop location (one open at a time): `open_pos_session` → `create_pos_sale` (an order of channel `store`, delivered at once, stock from the session's location, payments) → `close_pos_session` (counted vs `pos_expected_cash`, the difference booked as a movement). Receipts: `/admin/pos/receipt/[id]`. Shops are `locations` with `is_shop` (Stock settings); only they appear in Point of sale. Its Stock tab reads `?catalog=1` (a shop "carries" a variant once it has an `inventory_levels` row) and works with the till closed; its Analytics tab reads `GET /api/pos/analytics` (owners only: the income of every shop). **Till accounts:** a staff member with a shop (`app_metadata.location_id`, set in Admin → Team, `getShop()` in `lib/admin-auth.ts`, `RequestActor.shopId`) works only in that shop: `/api/pos` pins its catalog, tills, sessions, receipts and sales there and refuses another shop's with 403. Staff never see shop sales (channel `store`) in the Orders list. `locations.low_stock_limit` replaces the variants' `reorder_point` at that location for `notify_low_stock`, which also tells `pos` staff (audience `pos`) about shops, with a link to the Stock tab.
- **Purchasing.** `purchase_orders` (+ lines: variants, raw materials or free text; status `draft` / `rfq` price request / `ordered` / `partial` / `received` / `cancelled`) saved with `save_purchase_order`; goods arrive through `purchase_receipts` (accepted / rejected per line) and enter stock only when validated (`validate_purchase_receipt`, at the order's unit cost). `purchase_price_comparison()`. Raw materials have their own append-only `material_movements`; cost price per model = bill of materials (`product_bom`) + `product_costing` labor/overhead (`product_cost_prices()`); `record_production` consumes materials and restocks finished units at cost price.
- **Accounting (Tunisian SCE).** `accounts` (seeded chart; `is_system` ones are used by automatic entries, mapped in `erp_settings.accounting.accounts`), `journal_entries` + `journal_lines`. Triggers book entries with `private.book()` / `private.unbook()`: delivered order → sale (VT, prices include VAT at `sales_vat_rate`), payments (CA/BQ/OD), carrier payouts (fees → 6241), `bills` (AC; supplier invoices and expenses) and `bill_payments`, treasury movements, `ad_spend`, invoice stamp duty. Nothing is booked before `accounting.start_date`. Entries start as drafts; `post_journal_entries` numbers them per journal and year (gap-free `private.next_number`) and freezes them (`protect_posted_entry`); corrections are reversals. Purchases are expensed on their bill and `book_stock_variation` adjusts stock accounts at period end (intermittent inventory). Reports: `trial_balance`, `vat_summary`, and `/api/finance/reports` (ledger, income statement, balance sheet, FEC-style CSV export). `sales_invoices` (FA-/AV- numbers, `issue_invoice`, `cancel_invoice` by credit note) never change once issued. `bank_statement_lines` + `auto_match_bank` reconcile the bank. `documents` live in the private `documents` bucket (S3, links signed for 5 minutes).
- **Owner overview and objectives.** `owner_overview(from, to, channel, agent, product, campaign)` feeds Admin → Business overview (`/api/owner/overview`, owners only): delivered sales, gross margin (from `order_items.unit_cost`), estimated net margin (− ads, bills except stock purchases, carrier fees), money collected, cash/bank/carrier, receivables, supplier debts, stock value, rates, and breakdowns. `business_goals` holds the whole business's goal per month (delivered sales, gross margin, orders taken/confirmed/delivered, highest return rate, ad budget), set in Admin → Objectives & commissions (`PUT /api/admin/goals`); `teamProgress()` in `api/src/admin/goals.ts` measures it with `owner_overview` over the month (Tunis time) and the pace so far, shown on that page, the Business overview and My day (`MonthGoalCard`). `agent_goals` (set with `set_agent_goal`) hold each member's monthly targets and commissions (`monthProgress()`); agents see theirs on My day (`/api/admin/my-work`). Commissions (`commissionFor()` in `lib/erp/objectives.ts`, used by `api/src/admin/goals.ts`): % of delivered sales, an amount per delivered order, or **prize tiers** (`agent_goals.prize_tiers`, e.g. 50 delivered orders → 100 TND, 80 → 200 TND: the highest tier reached pays). Objectives also show orders delivered per day and the prizes to pay. **Analytics reset:** Business settings → Analytics stores `erp_settings.analytics.start_date`; the Business overview, Agent performance, Objectives and My day count only from that date (`clampToStart()`); nothing is deleted, and clearing it shows the full history.
- **Team tasks.** `team_tickets` is the back office's board (Admin → Team tasks, `/admin/tickets`, `/api/admin/tickets`, `api/src/admin/tickets.controller.ts`), separate from `crm_tasks` (customer follow-ups, `customers` permission only). Any back-office user sees, edits, moves and assigns every ticket through the `/api/admin` rule; deleting needs its author or the `delete` permission. Status is `todo` / `in_progress` / `done` (`done_at` stamped by the API; the board keeps done tickets 30 days), priority `low` / `normal` / `high`, `due_date` a plain date, `ticket_number` the short key shown as T-12, `position` the place in its column (dragging with `@hello-pangea/dnd` sets it between the new neighbours). The board edits a ticket in place, in `app/admin/tickets/page.tsx`. The trigger `private.notify_ticket_assignee` tells a new assignee, unless they took it themselves. `team_ticket_comments` (`/api/admin/tickets/comments`) hold text and/or one image, stored in the private `documents` bucket (`tickets/<ticket id>/…`, links signed for an hour); `private.notify_ticket_comment` tells the assignee and the author. Owners also get "Delete done tasks" on the board (`DELETE ?ids=`, soft). `daily_alerts()` reminds each assignee (the author when nobody on the team has it) every morning until a ticket is done, and My day lists a person's open tickets (`tickets` on `/api/admin/my-work`). `adminHome(role)` (`lib/admin-auth.ts`, used by `middleware.ts`) starts staff on this board and owners and admins on the dashboard: after sign-in, at `/admin`, and when a page is refused.
- **Alerts.** `private.daily_alerts()` (run by NestJS every day at 07:00 UTC) notifies orders waiting too long, late deliveries, follow-ups due, team tasks overdue or due today, bills due within 3 days and expiring documents; low stock (`notify_low_stock`) and incoming customer messages notify immediately.
